# ECE Development Project

**Electrical Engineering · Applied Learning · Living Technical Portfolio**

## Mission

I am developing practical, transferable engineering skills alongside my Electrical Engineering studies at North Carolina State University. My aim is to turn classroom knowledge into tested systems and sound engineering judgment through hands-on projects.

This is both my working engineering repository and a public record of what I design, build, test, troubleshoot, and learn.

## Engineering Philosophy

> **Design it simply. Build it correctly. Test it rigorously. Expect it to break. Make it easy to fix.**

My experience as a U.S. Air Force F-15 crew chief taught me that successful designs must consider reliability, maintainability, accessibility, and the technicians responsible for keeping them operational.

I see the engineering process as a cycle of learning: **define → build → test → analyze → improve**. Every success makes the failures valuable.
